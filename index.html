About Sue B's Hair Boutique

Dedicated to Making You Look & Feel Extraordinary

At Sue B's Hair Boutique, LLC, styling hair is more than a routine—it's an art form tailored to your unique lifestyle, personality, and hair texture. Located conveniently in Crescent, PA, Sue offers a warm, personal atmosphere where every client receives undivided focus and top-tier expertise.

Whether you are coming in for a vivid color overhaul, a smoothing Brazilian Blowout, precise texturizing, or a haircut for the entire family, Sue utilizes premium salon-grade formulas to maintain the ultimate shine, strength, and health of your hair.
